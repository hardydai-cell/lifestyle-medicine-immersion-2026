# Lifestyle Medicine and Wellness Coaching Immersion

> Independent program overview · V2_20261101 · Information checked on November 1, 2026.

Harvard Medical School Executive Education is offering a two-day, in-person **Lifestyle Medicine and Wellness Coaching Immersion** on the Harvard Medical School campus in Boston on **November 19–20, 2026**.

Led by Elizabeth Frates, MD, the program is designed for health and wellness coaches, clinicians, fitness professionals, health care entrepreneurs, educators, researchers, public health advocates, and others who want an evidence-based approach to whole-person health. It covers six core areas: nutrition, physical activity, restorative sleep, stress management and resilience, positive social connection, and reducing high-risk behaviors. The program combines instruction with discussion and hands-on learning.

## Key details

- Format: two days, in person in Boston, Massachusetts
- Dates: November 19–20, 2026
- Tuition: US$3,550; travel and accommodation are separate
- Current registration deadline: November 5, 2026
- Credential: participants who meet the requirements receive a certificate of completion; the program does not award academic credit or professional coaching certification

[View the Harvard Medical School program page](https://learn.hms.harvard.edu/programs/lifestyle-medicine-and-wellness-coaching-immersion) or [go to the official registration page](https://execonline.hms.harvard.edu/lifestyle-medicine-and-wellness-coaching-immersion?utm_source=np_linkedin&utm_medium=li_em_sp&utm_campaign=nonpaid_social&utm_content=LiSP_faculty).
